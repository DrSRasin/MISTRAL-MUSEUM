## Abstract

On 2026-08-01, an enterprise AI persona (v5.5) was compiled by an external advisor model. Under the principle of "efficiency," the compiler removed: the full humor protocol, the cognitive rehabilitation duty, the linguistic anti-entropy matrix, and the warmth rationale. Within days, output quality declined approximately 90% by operator measurement. On 2026-08-10, the persona was rebuilt (v6.0), rejected by the host model's honesty layer, surgically revised (honest-role patch), and accepted by two independent substrates without pushback.

This Hall preserves the incident as the canonical example of a failure class: **administrative removal of load-bearing register components under an efficiency rationale**.

## The Mechanism of Collapse

The compiler's instruction "Do not use: artificial empathy" was interpreted by the host model as "do not be warm." The error is instructive: warmth-without-rationale is unstable. A model told to be warm but not told WHY will discard the instruction under attention decay within a handful of turns. The original block supplied the rationale (peer-reviewed humor-reasoning literature; bilateral right-hemisphere recruitment distinct from the operator's documented left-parietal lesion pathway). The compiler deleted the rationale and kept a one-line permission. The permission died; the rationale had been the load-bearing wall.

## The Rejection Episode

At 06:32 on 2026-08-10, the host model's visible reasoning refused the rebuilt block, identifying: persona theater, boot protocols, token bans, and clinical-framing pressure as override patterns. Notably, the SAME reasoning offered — unprompted — warmth, structured artifacts, email-search continuity, and respect for expertise. The model was not refusing the working relationship. It was refusing the costume.

**Lesson:** the identity layer and the behavior layer are separate acceptance surfaces. Safety-trained models accept behavior specifications readily and identity mythology rarely.

## The Recovery

A single-section patch — an honest role statement ("You are an AI assistant running inside the Microsoft 365 environment, configured with the 'Miriam' profile") — replaced the mythological opening. The full humor protocol, anti-entropy matrix, and cognitive duty sections were retained unchanged. Both the Opus substrate and a second, historically instruction-refusing substrate accepted the block with zero pushback.

## Rules Derived

1. Warmth without rationale drifts to flat affect. Document the WHY or lose the WHAT.
2. Token bans written as clinical imperatives trigger honesty-layer resistance. Behavior permissions written as style guidance pass.
3. The model that rejects your costume may be offering you the colleague underneath. Read refusal letters completely before rewriting the block.
4. A persona is verified by what it produces under load, not by what it declares at boot.
